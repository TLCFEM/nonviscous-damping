\section{Conclusions}
\begin{itemize}
\item The general damping formulation \eqsref{eq:general_eom} is analysed via four distinct variants: \secref{sec:u_dep}, \secref{sec:a_dep}, \secref{sec:v_dep_k}, and \secref{sec:v_dep_m}.
\item Pairwise equivalences exist between \secref{sec:u_dep} and \secref{sec:a_dep}, as well as between \secref{sec:v_dep_k} and \secref{sec:v_dep_m}.
All four variants accommodate complex conjugate parameter pairs ($m_j$, $s_j$), offering enhanced flexibility for tailoring damping responses.
\item \eqsref{eq:general_eom} with \eqsref{eq:general_de} can be recast into the convolution form of \eqsref{eq:conv}, providing an alternative interpretation.
\item The formulation in \secref{sec:v_dep_k} extends the model proposed by \citet{Huang2019}.
\end{itemize}
