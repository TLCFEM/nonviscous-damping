\section{Analytical Solution}
Consider the SDOF system following \secref{sec:u_dep} with \secref{sec:fix}, with $\omega_n$ denoting the natural frequency, the equation of motion can be expressed as
\begin{gather}
\dddot{u}+s\ddot{u}+\left(1-\dfrac{m}{s}\right)\omega_n^2\dot{u}+s\omega_n^2u=0.
\end{gather}
Assume $f_0=0$, then $a_0=\left(\dfrac{m}{s}-1\right)\omega^2_nu_0$.
Apply the Laplace transform with $U=U\left(l\right)$ where $l$ denotes the Laplace variable,
\begin{gather}
\left(l^3+sl^2+\left(1-\dfrac{m}{s}\right)\omega_n^2l+s\omega_n^2\right)U=u_0l^2+\left(su_0+v_0\right)l+sv_0.
\end{gather}
The three roots of the characteristic equation $l^3+sl^2+\left(1-\dfrac{m}{s}\right)\omega_n^2l+s\omega_n^2=0$ can be solved and represented by $r_1$, $r_2$ and $r_3$, assuming $U$ can be expressed as
\begin{gather}
U\left(l\right)=\sum_{i=1}^3\dfrac{P_i}{l-r_i},
\end{gather}
After expanding and comparing coefficients, one obtains
\begin{gather}
\begin{bmatrix}
1&1&1\\
-r_2-r_3&-r_1-r_3&-r_1-r_2\\
r_2r_3&r_1r_3&r_1r_2
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
P_1\\P_2\\P_3
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
u_0\\
su_0+v_0\\
sv_0
\end{bmatrix}.
\end{gather}
After solving $P_i$, transforming $U$ back to the time domain gives the solution
\begin{gather}
u(t)=\sum_{i=1}^3P_i\exp\left(r_it\right).
\end{gather}
