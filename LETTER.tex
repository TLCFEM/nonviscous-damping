\opening{To Whom It May Concern,}
I am writing to express my strong interest in the Structural Engineer position at Mynaric. With over a decade of experience in computational mechanics and structural dynamics, including extensive academic research work on numerical methods of various problems, I am eager to apply my expertise in computational solid mechanics, finite element analysis (FEA) and structural optimization to the development of Mynaric's projects.

The challenge of defining the opto-mechanical architecture for terminals requires a unique synthesis of precision and resilience. My background in nonlinear dynamics provides me with the rigorous analytical tools required to solve complex structural mechanics problems, including the calculation of dynamic, static, and shock loads encountered during the launch phase of LEO satellite constellations. My Ph.D. research focused on the numerical simulation of structures under transient loading, an experience that directly translates to the aerospace requirements for random vibration and shock response analysis.

I am an expert in Finite Element Analysis, having developed the suanPan framework --- an open-source, parallel, and heterogeneous FEA platform written in modern C++. This experience goes beyond the standard use of software; it has provided me with a deep, code-level understanding of numerical solvers and integration schemes. I am highly proficient in the mathematical foundations of the corresponding numerical methods, and have broad interests and exposure to various topics in this field.

In addition to structural dynamics, I possess specialized knowledge in constitutive modelling. I have researched and implemented advanced constitutive models for various types of materials, which is essential for driving the thermoelastic behaviour of optical components in the extreme thermal environment of orbit. Furthermore, I also have certain exposure to topology optimization and the popular algorithms in this field, which can be applied to the design of lightweight and efficient structures for space applications.

During my tenure at various universities, I have demonstrated a consistent ability to work independently while contributing effectively to multidisciplinary, international project teams. I am a results-oriented professional who values clear communication and the implementation of best practices in engineering development. Having lived and worked in Berlin since 2022, I am well-integrated into the German professional landscape and communicate fluently in English, with a strong commitment to further developing my German language skills.

I am eager to discuss how my technical depth in structural dynamics and computational mechanics can help define the next generation of laser communication terminals.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
